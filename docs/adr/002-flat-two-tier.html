ADR 002 — Flat two-tier

Status: accepted · foundational

Context

Classic multi-agent orchestration stacks tiers: Master → Manager → Conductor → Agent. Those middle tiers earn their keep only under specific pressure: a Manager daemon assigns work across many tasks (breadth / multi-tenant), and a Conductor is a per-task resident session that babysits weaker workers. Neither pressure applies to running one epic with a capable model.

Decision

OOD is flat: lead + role teammates. The lead is Master and Conductor merged. No Manager, no Conductor tier.

Consequences

  • Simpler: fewer handoffs, less digest-loss, a thin lead that routes and gates.
  • Depth, not breadth: one epic at a time with internal parallelism. Many concurrent epics is out of scope — that's where a coordinator tier would return.
  • Aligned with the substrate: Agent Teams forbids nesting, so >2 tiers is impossible anyway. The constraint and the choice agree.

Alternatives considered

  • Keep a Conductor tier — rejected: a middle manager with no job at single-epic scale.
  • Nested Agent Teams — impossible: teammates cannot spawn their own teams.
